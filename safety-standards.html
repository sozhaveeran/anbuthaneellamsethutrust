Child Safety Standards for ATEST

Effective Date: October 2025

1. Prohibition of CSAE

We strictly prohibit any content that involves child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE). Users sharing such content will be immediately banned.

2. Monitoring and Content Moderation

All user-generated content is actively monitored. We use automated filters and manual review to detect inappropriate content.

3. Reporting Mechanism

Users can report any inappropriate content via:

  • In-app report button
  • Email: safety@yourcompany.com

4. Contact Information

Email: safety@yourcompany.com
